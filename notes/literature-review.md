# Literature and contribution boundary

Review date: 17 October 2026. The review is a targeted search, not proof of universal novelty or a patent search.

## Sources inspected

1. Weil, *Numbers of solutions of equations in finite fields* (1949), DOI 10.1090/S0002-9904-1949-09219-4. The diagonal character expansion and Hasse–Davenport lifting are classical. Accessible university-hosted text was inspected.
2. Gouvêa and Yui, *Arithmetic of Diagonal Hypersurfaces over Finite Fields* (1995), DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511526060. The 1994 MPIM preprint and Cambridge excerpt were inspected. Twisting vectors and twisted Jacobi sums are established. The proposed paper is a specific comparison of plane descent within this framework.
3. Degtyarev, Itenberg, Ottem, *Planes in cubic fourfolds* (2023), DOI 10.14231/ag-2023-007. The 405-plane Fermat configuration is established. The complex extremal theorem is not transferred to positive characteristic; the paper supplies a direct Hessian argument in characteristic not 2 or 3.
4. Auel, Kulkarni, Petok, Weinbaum, *A census of cubic fourfolds over F2* (2025), DOI 10.1090/mcom/4010. Its scope already includes point, line, and plane statistics and zeta functions. This rules out claiming that jointly storing or comparing these invariants is a new research area. The new artifact is a small odd-characteristic symbolic family and a ten-class diagonal comparison.
5. Addington and Auel, *Some non-special cubic fourfolds* (2018), DOI 10.4171/DM/628. The paper does not challenge cohomological conclusions from point counting; it distinguishes a cycle class from a rational plane representative.
6. Debarre, Laface, Roulleau, *Lines on cubic hypersurfaces over finite fields*, corrected arXiv:1510.05803v5. The line-count relation is established and credited. The same-zeta conclusion for line varieties is an application, not a newly discovered relation.
7. Shioda and Katsura, *On Fermat varieties* (1979), DOI 10.2748/tmj/1178229881. Context for Fermat cycles, not the source of a claimed new general theorem about them.

## Queries used

Searches included “diagonal cubic fourfolds zeta”, “cubic fourfolds same zeta planes”, “diagonal cubic same zeta function”, “Fermat fourfold twists finite fields planes”, and coefficient-pattern variants. No source explicitly stating this pair was located in the inspected material. This does not prove that the pair or an equivalent formulation has never appeared.

## Defensible claim

An explicit theorem comparing the twists (1,1,1,1,a,a²) and (1,1,a,a,a²,a²), a complete calculation of their plane-scheme Frobenius actions, and a reproducible diagonal classification over F7. All-extension equality is proved using classical formulas; it is not inferred from finite experiments.

## Personal prior-work overlap audit

First pages of 89 main PDFs in the user-authorized research directory were read. None matched the terms fourfold, Fermat, Jacobi sum, or rational plane. The nearest filename, Frobenius Lens, concerns periodic cellular automata rather than diagonal hypersurfaces. Other ICERM manuscripts concern numerical linear algebra, kinetic compression, and algebraic distance certificates. This is a topic and abstract-level audit, not a line-by-line comparison of every manuscript.

## Software/application landscape

The existing cubic-census repository and Sage scheme-count interfaces provide concrete integration contexts. A regression fixture that detects transferring incidence data across equal zeta functions is useful in computational research. No customer research, market sizing, willingness-to-pay study, or patentability assessment has been conducted. Those claims are deliberately absent.
